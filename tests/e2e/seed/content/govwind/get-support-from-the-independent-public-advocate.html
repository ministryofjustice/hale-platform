If you or someone you know is in danger, please call 999. If it is not an emergency but you need help from the police, please call 101.

Victims of a major incident can request support from the Independent Public Advocate (IPA).

Get support

Victim support organisations

If you are a victim of a crime seeking support, there are many specialist organisations offering information, advice and support.

  • The Victim and Witness Information website helps victims and witnesses of crime in England and Wales to find support in their local area.
  • If you are going to court as a victim or witness, you can get advice and support from the Citizens Advice Witness Service.
