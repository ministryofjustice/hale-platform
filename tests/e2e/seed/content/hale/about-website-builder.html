About Website Builder

We help teams across the Ministry of Justice (MoJ) and its arm’s length bodies create and maintain consistent, accessible and secure websites.

We do this using Website Builder – our WordPress publishing tool that allows us to host all sites on one platform.

We make ongoing improvements to the platform and add new publishing features designed to enhance the user experience.

Why choose Website Builder?

It’s free of charge

Our team is funded centrally in Justice Digital, and the platform is hosted on MoJ’s Cloud Platform, so there are no upfront or ongoing costs.

Secure and robust

Your website will always be on an up-to-date version of WordPress and is securely hosted on the MoJ Cloud Platform infrastructure.

An expert, multi-disciplinary team

We are expert developers, content designers, interaction designers and user researchers.

Flexible designs

You can structure each page using flexible content components called blocks. Sites can be customised with your organisation’s branding and colours.

Accessible and consistent

The platform helps you to meet accessibility regulations. Platform features are based on the GOV.UK Design System.

You’re in control

We’ll provide all the guidance and training you need to build your site content, and support you once it is live.
